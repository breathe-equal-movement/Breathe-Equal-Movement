Take Action

Change happens when
people demand it.

You don't need to be a scientist or a lawyer to make a difference. Here are concrete, proven ways to push for environmental justice — starting today.

Four Ways to Help

Pick your level of involvement.

Whether you have five minutes or five hours, there is something meaningful you can do. Start small and build from there.

01
Sign & Share the Petition
The simplest first step. Our petition calls on Congress to strengthen enforcement of the Clean Air Act and the Safe Drinking Water Act in underserved communities. Every signature counts.
Sign the Petition →
02
Contact Your Representatives
Call or email your US Senator or House Representative. It takes under 10 minutes and it works. We've written a template letter you can personalize and send in seconds.
Use Our Template →
03
Donate to Frontline Organizations
Support the nonprofits doing the hard legal, scientific, and community work on the ground. Even a small monthly donation makes a sustained impact. See our curated list below.
See Organizations →
04
Spread the Word
Awareness is power. Share this website with your network, post about environmental justice on social media, and bring these conversations to your school, church, or community group.

Organizations to Support

The people doing the work.

These nonprofits are leading the fight for environmental justice. Consider donating or volunteering.

Earthjustice
The nation's largest environmental law nonprofit — using the courts to hold polluters and government agencies accountable in communities that can't fight alone.
earthjustice.org →
NAACP Environmental & Climate Justice
Connecting environmental justice to the broader civil rights movement, with specific programs targeting clean air, clean water, and energy equity.
naacp.org →
WE ACT for Environmental Justice
Based in Northern Manhattan, WE ACT builds community power and advocates for fair environmental protection policies for people of color and low-income residents.
weact.org →
Clean Air Task Force
Science-driven advocacy targeting the pollution sources that harm frontline communities most, including heavy industry, methane, and diesel emissions.
catf.us →
Toxic-Free Future
Focused on eliminating toxic chemicals in consumer products and environments — with specific programs supporting low-income communities facing the highest exposure.
toxicfreefuture.org →
Navajo Water Project
Bringing clean water systems to Navajo families who have lived without running water for generations — a direct response to environmental abandonment by the US government.
navajowaterproject.org →

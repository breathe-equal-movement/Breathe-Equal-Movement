About

One student.
One urgent cause.

Breathe Equal was created by a 13-year-old eighth-grade student who believes that the zip code you're born into should never determine the quality of air you breathe, water you drink, or land you call home.

My Story

Why I built this.

I am a 13-year-old eighth-grade student passionate about social justice, environmental science, and health equity. I created Breathe Equal because I believe that some of the most serious problems in America are also some of the most invisible — not because the evidence isn't there, but because the people most affected often lack the platform to make their voices heard.

When I first learned about environmental health inequity — that where you live can determine whether your children develop asthma, whether your water is safe to drink, whether your neighborhood sits next to a Superfund site — I felt a moral obligation to do something. This website is that something.

I am not an expert. I am not a scientist or a lawyer. But I am a young person who cares deeply, who has done the research, and who believes that awareness is the first step toward justice. I built every page of this site myself and I stand behind every fact on it.

My goal is simple: to help people understand a crisis that affects millions of their fellow Americans, and to give them the tools to act on that understanding.

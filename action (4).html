Organizations to Support

The people doing the work.

These nonprofits are leading the fight for environmental justice. Consider donating or volunteering.

Earthjustice
The nation's largest environmental law nonprofit — using the courts to hold polluters and government agencies accountable.
earthjustice.org →
NAACP Environmental & Climate Justice
Connecting environmental justice to the broader civil rights movement, with programs targeting clean air, water, and energy equity.
naacp.org →
WE ACT for Environmental Justice
Builds community power and advocates for fair environmental protection policies for people of color and low-income residents.
weact.org →
Clean Air Task Force
Science-driven advocacy targeting pollution sources that harm frontline communities most.
catf.us →
Toxic-Free Future
Eliminating toxic chemicals in environments with specific programs supporting low-income communities facing the highest exposure.
toxicfreefuture.org →
Navajo Water Project
Bringing clean water systems to Navajo families who have lived without running water for generations.
navajowaterproject.org →
